04Things that hold attention

Outside the syllabus.

Current curiosities.

Not a definitive list. Just a few threads I keep picking up.

01Web Development & AI+

With the dawning age of vibecoding, web development has become more fun than ever, and I am in the process of learning how to integrate the two and find that balance. This website is to be the first of many efforts in that journey.

02Philosophy+

The thing about philosophy that I like is that it lets me debate arguably the smartest person I know: myself. It really makes you appreciate how little we understand about abstract concepts like free will, consciousness and love. Rational thought is humanity's biggest asset, and it is often underused.

03MUNs+

A more recent pastime, and one I feel has been very helpful in improving my public speaking skills. I haven't participated in a lot of MUNs yet, but I hope to change that over the next few years.

04Creature Comforts+

After a long day of college, there is nothing more satisfying than lounging back on my sofa with a bucket of KFC and binge-watching whatever show, anime or movie catches my eye. I'm a firm believer that no time spent in comfort is time wasted.

I'm also a huge geek when it comes to anime and TV shows. When I'm home, you'll probably find me either eating or absorbing some form of comfort media much more often than not.

05Gaming+

I don't really play a huge variety of games. Minecraft, Valorant, a few RPGs and indie games make up most of it. But I can't not mention the Pokémon craze I have going.

I've never understood how a guy can struggle to memorize the elements of the periodic table but, when asked about Pokémon, can name a good thousand of them along with their Pokédex numbers and base stats.

I've been playing competitive Pokémon for about six years now, and I would like to think I'm quite good at it. Somehow, I never got around to playing all of the original games though.

06Books+

Reading has been my favourite escape from reality since I was six. From the fictional worlds of Harry Potter, Percy Jackson, The Lord of the Rings and A Song of Ice and Fire, to Paulo Coelho, Dan Brown and Chetan Bhagat, my reading habits have never been particularly loyal to one genre.

Then there is the seemingly endless collection of romance novels: Nicholas Sparks, Colleen Hoover, and whoever else happens to find their way onto my bookshelf.

I'll read fantasy, mystery, romance, philosophy, or something completely ridiculous if it gives me a world I want to stay in.
