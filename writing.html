03Words, more or less

Writing / a slow practice

I like to think I am something of a self-proclaimed writer. Maybe I'm flattering myself with the title, but there's a strange sense of fulfilment that comes with putting thoughts into words.

01

Theism

I am not a particularly religious person but I do have faith and by writing about my experiences, I hope to answer the questions I had regarding the existence of an all-loving Creator during my own atheist and agnostic phases and hopefully make it easier for anyone else who would be interested.

02

Wattpad

Yeah I won't be giving away my wattpad tag here but you are welcome to try and find it. I dabbled in writing a lot of fan-fiction back in the day but those are far from presentable. You would be surprised with what you'd find.

03

Scarlet Star

My best work by far and a work in progress. What started as a self-insert power fantasy (don't get me wrong, it still is) in 2024 is now a 200+ page novel with 15-ish chapters and its only halfway done.
